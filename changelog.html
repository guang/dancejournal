What's New

Every release of Dance Journal, and what it means for your practice.

v0.13.0 August 7, 2026

One thread per clip, and a streak worth keeping

This release folds notes and feedback into a single thread on every clip, and gives your consistency somewhere to show up.

  • One thread per clip — your notes and a teacher's feedback now live in the same running thread, not separate lists.
  • Practice streak — a weekly streak band on Home tracks how consistently you're showing up.
  • Flip any video — mirror a clip for either viewer, on every clip you save or receive.
  • Cloud storage, redesigned — see what's using space and manage it clip by clip.
  • Ask for a review — share a public link that asks specifically for feedback on a clip.
v0.12.0 July 22, 2026

Discover, and a Home that gets out of your way

This release opens up a way to find other dancers' work, and clears out Home so it just shows you what's next.

  • Discover — browse public collections and schools, and request to join a class.
  • Home, redesigned — one clear hero card and a consistent set of stats, instead of a stack of staged sections.
  • Collections — group moves and clips into a shareable collection with its own public link.
  • Pick a goal on day one — new students set a monthly goal as part of onboarding.
  • Practice reminders — a nudge tied to your goals and classes, not just a generic push.
v0.11.0 June 30, 2026

A calmer look, and a place for every note

This release trades the old high-contrast look for something calmer, and gives every note you've ever left a home.

  • A calmer look — a top-to-bottom visual redesign, softer and easier to read at a glance.
  • My notes — a chronological journal of every note you've left, across every clip.
  • Feedback tab — Coaching is now Feedback, with a streamlined sheet for asking or sending it.
  • Role-aware onboarding — sign in as a student or a teacher and get a setup built for that role.
  • Faster multi-select — a persistent strip keeps your selected clips in view as you pick more.
v0.10.0 June 19, 2026

Set your goals, sharpen your feedback

This update helps you aim your practice and get more out of every piece of feedback.

  • Goals — set personal dance goals and watch your progress build toward them.
  • Feedback, all in one place — teacher feedback and your own notes now live together on each clip, with a redesigned review flow.
  • Draw on your videos — mark up your take recordings with on-screen annotations as you study a move.
  • Practice progression — see how a move has developed across your sessions.
  • Dial in the tempo — a finer speed slider slows a move down to exactly the right pace.
v0.9.0 June 1, 2026

Bring clips in from anywhere

This release opens your library up to the videos you already learn from — and makes it faster to get back to them.

  • Import from Instagram — paste a reel link and it lands in your library, ready to study.
  • Share to Dance Notes — send a clip straight in from Instagram's share sheet.
  • Recent rails — jump right back to what you were just working on, in both Library and Discover.
  • Streamlined Discover — a cleaner split between tutorials and your own practice recordings.
v0.8.2 May 29, 2026

Sharper feedback, clearer progress

This update is all about making your clips easier to compare, your feedback easier to refine, and your library easier to move through.

  • Compare and link in one flow — line your clip up against a tutorial or a past recording without breaking your stride.
  • Edit your feedback notes — long-press any message in a review thread to fix a typo or reword a note.
  • Tidier clip controls — delete moved to the top-level options menu, with a smoother progress-stage picker.
  • Discover, renamed and refreshed — Home is now Discover, with a clearer split between tutorials and your own practice recordings.
  • Follow your dance friends — follow notifications, an "added you" banner, and quick invite sharing.

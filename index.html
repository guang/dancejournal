How it works

1

Start with a clip

A tutorial you saved on Instagram, or the combo you filmed in class last night. Share it straight in, or pick it from your camera roll.

2

Drop it into a goal

A goal is just a short list of what you're working on right now — a few moves, and a date you pick. It's what keeps the clip from becoming another saved-and-forgotten video.

3

Loop it, then film it

Mark the bit you keep botching — usually four 8-counts — and put it on repeat. Then record your try, right against it.

4

Spot the difference

Play your take and the tutorial side by side, frame for frame. The gap you couldn't feel is suddenly obvious.

5

Pin the fix

Circle the arm that's late. Drop an emoji, or write it out — right on the frame, at the exact second it goes wrong. Tap it next week and the video jumps straight back to the moment.

6

Move it up as it clicks

Every move sits on a three-rung board — Learning ○ → Polishing ◐ → Got it ●. Bump it up when it starts to feel right, and watch your goal fill in behind you.

+

Want another pair of eyes?

Send the clip to your teacher or a friend. Their notes land pinned to the exact frame — so this time the correction doesn't evaporate on the walk home.
